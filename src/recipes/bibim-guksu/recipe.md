# Bibim Guksu (Korean Spicy Cold Noodles)

Bibim guksu is a cold noodle dish with crisp fresh vegetables. The noodles are mixed in a spicy, sweet and sour gochujang sauce.

## Info

- Time
  - 10 mins
- Makes
  - 1 servings
- Macros/serving
  - 570 kcal
  - 19g protein
  - 92g carbs
  - 11g fat
- Tags
  - Pasta
  - Main course

## Ingredients

- 100g dried wheat noodles (somyeon noodles)
- 1 tbsp gochujang
- 1½ tbsp soy sauce
- 1 tbsp rice vinegar
- 1 tsp sugar
- 1 tsp toasted sesame oil
- 1 clove garlic, minced
- 1 tbsp water
- Handful of sliced cucumber
- 1 soft-boiled egg, halved
- Optional toppings: sliced carrots, kimchi, kimchi juice

## Instructions

1. Cook dried wheat noodles per package directions until al dente. Drain and rinse under cold water until cool, then drain well.
2. Prepare the sauce by combining gochujang, soy sauce, rice vinegar, sugar, toasted sesame oil, garlic and water. Whisk or stir until smooth.
3. Toss the chilled noodles with the sauce until evenly coated. Add a handful of sliced cucumber and any optional toppings like sliced carrots or kimchi.
4. Top with a soft-boiled egg (halved) and a drizzle of extra sesame oil or a splash of kimchi juice if you like more tang.
