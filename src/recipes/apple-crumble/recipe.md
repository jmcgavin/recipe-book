# Apple Crumble

A fall classic consisting of warm, tender apples topped with cinnamon and oats.

## Info

- Time
  - 1 hour
- Makes
  - ~8 servings
- Macros/serving
  - 340 kcal
  - 4g protein
  - 63g carbs
  - 9g fat
- Tags
  - Dessert

## Ingredients

- 2 cups brown sugar
- 2 cups quick oats
- ½ cup flour
- ⅓ cup butter
- ½ tsp nutmeg
- ½ tsp cinnamon
- ~6 apples (Granny Smith or another green, tart variety), chopped

## Instructions

1. Mix all ingredients.
2. Pour over chopped apples.
3. Bake at 375°F for 40 mins.

## Notes

- If your sweet tooth is calling for it, serve with maple syrup and a scoop of vanilla ice cream.
