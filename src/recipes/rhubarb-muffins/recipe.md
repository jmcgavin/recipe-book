# Rhubarb Muffins

Sweet and tart rhubarb muffins.

## Info

- Time
  - 30 mins
- Makes
  - 8 muffins
- Macros/serving
  - 240 kcal
  - 4g protein
  - 35g carbs
  - 10g fat
- Tags
  - Dessert

## Ingredients

- 1¼ cups flour
- ¼ cup granulated sugar
- ¼ cup brown sugar
- 1½ tsp baking powder
- 1 tsp salt
- 1 egg
- ½ cup milk
- ¼ cup vegetable oil
- 1 tsp almond flavouring
- 1½s cup diced rhubarb

## Instructions

1. Mix dry ingredients.
2. Beat egg, add milk, oil and almond flavouring.
3. Pour egg mixture over dry ingredients and stir to moisten. Do not over mix.
4. Fold in rhubarb. Do not over mix.
5. Bake at 375°F for 20 mins.
