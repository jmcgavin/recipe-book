# Brownies

Classic brownie recipe.

## Info

- Time
  - 45 mins
- Makes
  - 9 brownies
- Macros/serving
  - 300 kcal
  - 4g protein
  - 38g carbs
  - 16g fat
- Tags
  - Dessert

## Ingredients

- ⅓ cup Crisco (vegetable shortening)
- 4 tbsp butter
- 4 tbsp cocoa powder
- ½ tsp vanilla extract
- 1 cup granulated sugar
- 2 eggs
- ¾ cup flour
- ¼ tsp salt

## Instructions

1. Preheat oven to 350°F.
2. Melt the Crisco and butter. Add the cocoa and mix well.
3. Add the vanilla and sugar. Mix.
4. Add eggs, one at a time, mixing well after each.
5. Sift the flour and salt and add to the egg mix in 2-3 additions.
6. Spread brownie mix in an 8" x 8" mould.
7. Cook at 350°F for 25-30 mins.

## Notes

- This recipe works best with a dark, metal mould. Adjust temperature and cook time for different moulds.
- Insert a fork into the center of the brownies; they’re done when it comes out with a few moist crumbs attached but no wet batter.
