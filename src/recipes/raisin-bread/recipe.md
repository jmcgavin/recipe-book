# Raisin Bread

Mama's classic raisin bread.

## Info

- Time
  - 1 hour 30 mins
- Makes
  - ~10 slices
- Macros/serving
  - 275 kcal
  - 5g protein
  - 53g carbs
  - 6g fat
- Tags
  - Dessert

## Ingredients

- 1 cup boiling water
- 1 cup golden raisins
- ¼ cup Crisco (vegetable shortening)
- 1 cup granulated sugar
- 2 eggs
- 2¼ cups flour
- 3 tsp baking powder
- ½ tsp salt
- 1 tsp vanilla extract

## Instructions

1. In a bowl, add the boiling water to the raisins and let cool.
2. Preheat oven to 350°F.
3. In a large bowl add the Crisco. In 2-3 additions, add the sugar and beat well after each addition.
4. Add the eggs, one at a time, beating well after each.
5. Add the vanilla to the raisins.
6. Mix together the flour, baking powder and salt.
7. Add the dry flour mixture to the wet ingredients, alternating with raisins and mix until well combined.
8. Bake in a 9" x 5" x 3" loaf pan at 350°F for 1 hour.

## Notes

- Try a slice, toasted with butter. You won't regret it.
