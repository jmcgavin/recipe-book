# Italian Chicken Soup

A comforting soup with Italian seasonings, chicken meatballs, pasta, tomatoes, and zucchini simmered in a savory Parmesan broth.

## Info

- Time
  - 30 mins
- Makes
  - 4 servings
- Macros/serving
  - 530 kcal
  - 43g protein
  - 48g carbs
  - 18g fat
- Tags
  - Soup
  - Main Course

## Ingredients

- 1 egg
- 1 tsp + ½ tsp dried basil
- ½ tsp salt
- ½ cup finely-grated Parmesan cheese
- 2 900ml cartons of chicken broth
- ½ tsp dried oregano
- ½ tsp dried thyme
- 2 cups of bite-size pasta
- 454g ground chicken
- 2 large tomatoes, chopped
- 1 zucchini, chopped

## Instructions
 
1. In a large bowl crack an egg and sprinkle with ½ tsp of dried basil, salt and Parmesan cheese. Whisk until mixed.
2. Crumble in the ground chicken and use a fork or your hands to mix the chicken and until evenly combined.
3. Pour broth into a large pot set over high heat.
4. Add oregano, thyme and 1 tsp of of dried basil and bring to a boil.
5. When the broth is boiling, stir in the pasta and reduce heat to medium.
6. With wet hands, form chicken mixture into small bite-sized pieces (about the size of a cherry) and drop right into the simmering broth as each ball is formed. Or simply drop small pieces of meat right into the broth.
7. Add the tomatoes and zucchini and continue to gently boil until pasta and vegetables are cooked through. Total cooking time for pasta will be at least 10 mins.
